ABOUT RPM

RPM: Rapid Plus Math is an arcade speed-arithmetic game built to test and accelerate mental calculation speed under pressure.

🎮 How to Play

  • Hit Start Game to begin the 60-second countdown.
  • Solve the big equation displayed on screen.
  • Type the result in the box and press Enter ↵.
  • Each correct answer earns +1 point.
  • Climb the board and beat your all-time high score!
