ABOUT RPM

RPM: Rapid Plus Math is an arcade speed-arithmetic game built to test how quickly your brain calculates under a 60-second timer.

🎮 How to Play

  • Hit Start to trigger the 60s countdown.
  • Solve the big equation displayed on screen.
  • Type your answer and press Enter to instantly submit.
  • Each correct solution adds +1 to your score.
